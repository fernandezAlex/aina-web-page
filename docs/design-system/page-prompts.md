# Page Prompts

Use these prompts to generate page-level explorations in Figma AI, Stitch, Claude Design, or similar tools.

## Global instruction to prepend

Use this before any page-specific prompt:

```text
Design for Si Asha Foundation, a nonprofit institutional and fundraising website focused on children and young people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in Nepal.

Brand tone: hopeful, serious, warm, trustworthy, institutional but human.
Avoid generic NGO aesthetics, pity-based storytelling, startup SaaS styling, or overdecorated layouts.

Use this color system:
- #BD0833 primary
- #EB6B2F accent
- #FFA952 accent soft
- #9C5E03 earth tone
- #E8CEB0 warm surface
- #FFFFFF base
- #111111 text primary
- #333333 text secondary

Use real-photo-driven design, strong hierarchy, clear CTAs, spacious layout, and mobile-friendly structure.
```

## Home

```text
Create a homepage for Si Asha Foundation.

Goals:
- explain who Si Asha is immediately
- communicate the mission in Nepal
- show impact and programmes quickly
- build trust
- route users toward Donate, Become a Member, Volunteer, or Contact

Include these sections:
- hero with 1 primary CTA and 1-2 secondary CTAs
- problem/mission context
- impact stats band
- programme cards
- location or centres section
- trust/transparency preview
- strong final CTA band

The page should feel institutional, emotionally resonant, and highly clear.
```
