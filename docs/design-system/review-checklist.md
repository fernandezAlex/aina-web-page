# Review Checklist

Use this checklist to review designs created in Figma, Stitch, Claude Design, or other tools.

## Brand fit

- Does it feel like Si Asha and not a generic NGO?
- Does it feel hopeful, serious, warm, and trustworthy?
- Does it avoid startup or SaaS styling?
- Does it avoid pity-based storytelling?

## Visual system

- Are the official colors used correctly?
- Is red the dominant identity color?
- Is orange used as an accent, not as a competing primary?
- Is the layout spacious and readable?
- Is the typography hierarchy strong?

## Content hierarchy

- Is the mission understandable quickly?
- Are impact, programmes, and action paths clear?
- Are trust and transparency visible enough?
- Is there a clear next action?

## Page logic

- Does the page follow the intended page role from the IA?
- Are support paths clearly distinct when relevant?
- Are team and trust sections structured rather than decorative?

## Photography and imagery

- Are real images used appropriately?
- Do images preserve dignity and context?
- Is photography helping understanding, not just decoration?

## Forms and conversion

- Do forms look calm and trustworthy?
- Is payment-method choice clear?
- Is the primary CTA obvious?
- Does conversion feel dignified rather than manipulative?

## Responsive quality

- Does the design translate well to mobile?
- Is the hierarchy still clear on small screens?
- Do sections collapse sensibly?

## Trust and factual safety

- Has the design avoided inventing unresolved facts?
- Are unverified addresses avoided?
- Are missing legal documents not falsely represented as available?

## Final approval questions

- Would this screen be easy to translate into a consistent Figma system?
- Would this screen be easy to implement using the current design tokens and patterns?
- Does this screen strengthen the institutional credibility of the foundation?
