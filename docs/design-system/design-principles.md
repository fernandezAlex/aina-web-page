# Design Principles

## Purpose

These principles define what good web design looks like for `Si Asha Foundation`.

## Core principles

### Hope with weight

The website should feel hopeful, but not naive. It should communicate that hope is built through work, structure, education, therapy, and care.

### Warmth with credibility

The visual language must feel human and welcoming while still supporting institutional trust.

### Emotion with clarity

The site should move people emotionally, but never at the cost of legibility, hierarchy, or factual understanding.

### Dignity before drama

Children, families, staff, and communities must be represented with dignity. Do not build layouts that depend on spectacle, pity, or visualized suffering.

### Structure over novelty

Use strong hierarchy, consistent spacing, and disciplined pattern reuse. Do not chase novelty for its own sake.

### Trust is visible

Transparency, team, governance, contact, and programme specifics should be easy to find and visually credible.

## Layout principles

- clean section rhythm
- generous whitespace
- clear reading widths
- strong heading hierarchy
- obvious CTA hierarchy
- stable responsive behavior

## Visual composition principles

- use large real photography with purpose
- combine editorial calm with fundraising clarity
- keep backgrounds mostly light and breathable
- use bold color moments deliberately, not continuously

## Conversion principles

- ask clearly
- do not pressure emotionally
- make support paths distinct
- reduce friction in forms and payment choice

## Anti-principles

- no generic NGO landing-page look
- no startup SaaS visual language
- no decorative overload
- no cluttered one-page information dumping
- no weak hierarchy hidden behind pretty imagery
