# Figma / Stitch Master Brief

Use this as the master context brief when generating visual explorations in Figma AI, Google Stitch, Claude Design, or any similar design-generation tool.

## Project

Design a new official website for `Si Asha Foundation`.

This is a nonprofit institutional and fundraising website focused on children and young people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in Nepal.

The site must:

- explain the mission clearly
- present programmes and centres
- build trust through transparency and governance
- convert visitors into donors, members, volunteers, or contacts

## Brand direction

The brand should feel:

- hopeful
- serious
- warm
- human
- credible
- institutional but not cold
- emotionally strong but never manipulative

The website should not feel like:

- a generic NGO landing page
- a startup SaaS site
- a volunteer tourism project
- a pity-based charity campaign

## Mission frame

Si Asha exists to make education, therapy, care, protection, and inclusion real for children and young people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in Nepal.

## Tone and narrative constraints

- focus on dignity, rights, inclusion, long-term support, and real transformation
- avoid saviour narratives
- avoid victimizing language
- avoid vague inspirational fluff without structure or proof
- combine emotion with institutional trust

## Core pages

The approved top-level information architecture is:

- `Home`
- `Who We Are`
- `What We Do`
- `Get Involved`
- `Transparency`
- `Team & Board`
- `Contact`

`Get Involved` must support:

- `Exclusive Member`
- `General Member`
- `Donate`
- `Volunteer`

## Visual style

### Overall look

- editorial charity design
- strong real photography
- generous spacing
- clear section rhythm
- strong hierarchy
- institutional clarity with emotional warmth

### Photography

- use real documentary-style photography
- show classrooms, therapy, staff, families, care, and real environments
- portraits must feel dignified and respectful
- do not use stock-photo NGO imagery

## Corporate colors

Use these colors as the brand system:

- primary: `#BD0833`
- accent: `#EB6B2F`
- accent soft: `#FFA952`
- earth: `#9C5E03`
- warm surface: `#E8CEB0`
- base white: `#FFFFFF`
- text primary: `#111111`
- text secondary: `#333333`

Usage rules:

- red is the main identity and CTA color
- orange is a controlled accent, not a second dominant brand color
- warm beige can be used for selected soft backgrounds
- transparency and trust sections must remain clear and sober

## Typography direction

- headings: strong, clean, geometric, modern
- body: highly readable, human, calm
- short powerful headlines
- comfortable body spacing
- must work in Spanish and English

## Layout rules

- mobile-first but strong on desktop
- clean, spacious sections
- readable text widths
- cards and sections should feel ordered, not busy
- trust and documentation should be easy to scan

## Core patterns to reuse

- institutional hero
- impact stats band
- programme cards
- location / centre cards
- transparency document list
- team / board layout
- CTA band
- support option cards
- story block
- form shell

## Core facts to preserve

- `150+` children and young people receive education and therapy every day
- `3` Asha Schools & Rehabilitation Centres
- `1` Asha Residential Hostel
- `700+` families supported
- the first Asha School started in `2012` in `Hetauda`
- long-term vision: province-by-province expansion across Nepal

## Content and factual guardrails

Do not invent:

- exact school addresses
- missing transparency documents
- unresolved canonical contact email
- unverified team details

If something is missing, leave it structurally represented but not filled with invented content.

## Conversion priorities

The website must make these actions clear and visually distinct:

- become an exclusive member
- become a general member
- donate
- volunteer
- contact

Calls to action should be:

- direct
- dignified
- clear
- not guilt-driven

## Form and support-flow design constraints

- forms should feel calm and trustworthy
- payment method choice must be clear
- donation and membership flows must be distinct
- avoid overwhelming long single-block forms visually
- step-based or grouped-field logic is preferred

## What good output looks like

The final design direction should communicate:

- a real organisation
- a strong mission
- a trustworthy structure
- a warm but rigorous brand
- a clear path to action

## What to deliver when exploring

When generating a page or layout, prioritize:

- visual hierarchy
- section composition
- CTA structure
- mobile adaptability
- consistency with the token system and page patterns
