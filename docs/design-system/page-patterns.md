# Page Patterns

## Purpose

These are reusable structural patterns for major sections of the website.

## Hero pattern

Use on:

- home
- who we are
- what we do
- support paths

Required elements:

- eyebrow optional
- strong title
- short supporting text
- 1 primary CTA
- 1-2 optional secondary actions
- real photography or intentional brand-led image treatment

## Impact stats band

Use for:

- high-value proof moments

Rules:

- 3 to 5 metrics maximum in one row/group
- large number first
- short label second
- do not bury in heavy surrounding text

## Programme section

Use for:

- explaining what the foundation actually does

Rules:

- card or card-like rhythm
- image plus short summary
- avoid overlong text blocks inside cards

## Location section

Use for:

- Hetauda, Lalitpur/Kathmandu, Bardibas, future centres

Rules:

- show city/province clearly
- show key data points
- do not publish exact address unless verified

## Trust / transparency section

Use for:

- document libraries
- governance reassurance

Rules:

- minimal decoration
- strong hierarchy
- document metadata visible
- action to download or view obvious

## Team and board section

Use for:

- grouped people presentation

Rules:

- group by structure
- portrait grid or rows
- avoid collage aesthetics

## CTA band

Use for:

- strong conversion transitions between informational sections

Rules:

- one clear proposition
- no more than 3 actions
- strong color use allowed

## Support options pattern

Use for:

- exclusive member
- general member
- donate
- volunteer

Rules:

- each option must feel distinct
- financial amount or support type clear
- path should not require reading deep paragraphs to understand

## Story block

Use for:

- human proof

Rules:

- person, context, change
- dignity-first framing
- short narrative, not a full article on listing pages

## Form shell

Use for:

- all public forms

Rules:

- heading and expectation upfront
- grouped fields logically
- payment choice separated visually from personal details
- confirmation state explicit
