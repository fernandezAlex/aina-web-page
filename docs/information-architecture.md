# Information Architecture

This file defines the recommended sitemap and page model for the future official website.

## Primary sitemap

Approved core navigation from the internal brief:

- `Home`
- `Who We Are`
- `What We Do`
- `Get Involved`
- `Transparency`
- `Team & Board`
- `Contact`

## Recommended navigation model

### Primary navigation

- `Home`
- `Who We Are`
- `What We Do`
- `Get Involved`
- `Transparency`
- `Team & Board`
- `Contact`

### Persistent CTA options

At least one of these should remain easily accessible in the header or a sticky pattern:

- `Donate`
- `Become a Member`

## Recommended page breakdown

### Home

Role:

- front door for all audiences
- fast mission explanation
- fast action routing

Main exits:

- Who We Are
- What We Do
- Get Involved
- Transparency

### Who We Are

Role:

- define institutional identity
- explain origin, evolution, mission, vision, values

Optional subsections or anchors:

- story
- mission and vision
- values
- structure

### What We Do

Role:

- explain programmes and locations in practical terms

Optional subsections or anchors:

- education
- therapy
- residential care
- vocational inclusion
- family support
- where we work

### Get Involved

Role:

- hub for all support and participation actions

Recommended child pages or dedicated routes:

- `Exclusive Member`
- `General Member`
- `Donate`
- `Volunteer`

Optional future child pages:

- `Companies`
- `Sponsor / Apadrina`

### Transparency

Role:

- trust and accountability center

Recommended subsections:

- annual reports
- financial reports
- legal documents
- governance / ethics if available

### Team & Board

Role:

- make governance and leadership visible

Recommended sections:

- board of directors
- national operation team
- school management team

### Contact

Role:

- direct communication and trust touchpoint

Recommended sections:

- contact details
- form
- office / schools overview

## Priority user pathways

### Path A: trust-first visitor

`Home` -> `Who We Are` -> `Transparency` -> `Team & Board` -> `Get Involved`

### Path B: cause-first visitor

`Home` -> `What We Do` -> `Where We Work` section -> `Donate` or `Become a Member`

### Path C: action-first visitor

`Home` -> `Get Involved` -> specific support flow

## IA principles for future agents

- do not hide key information in a one-page scroll-only experience
- do not overload `Home` with every operational detail
- keep donation and membership routes distinct
- keep transparency visible at top-level navigation
- keep team/governance visible enough to support trust

## IA differences versus current public sites

Compared with `siashafoundation.org`, the new IA should:

- move from a single-page anchor layout to a clearer multi-page structure
- surface contact and team explicitly
- add structured support flows

Compared with `siasha.org`, the new IA should:

- stay disciplined around the approved internal top-level navigation
- borrow useful support routes and storytelling patterns where they help

## Content-to-page mapping summary

- history, mission, vision, values -> `Who We Are`
- programmes and centres -> `What We Do`
- donations, memberships, volunteer -> `Get Involved`
- legal and reports -> `Transparency`
- names, roles, portraits -> `Team & Board`
- contact methods and verified location information -> `Contact`
